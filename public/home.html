04 / Checks

Checks.

Design invariants. The five below are examples. I1–I8, and the checkers, are in the manual. Nothing here has been proven in this repository.

  • Acked bytes stay readable.
  • Offsets stay contiguous.
  • Retries do not duplicate.
  • No read before commit.
  • No referenced object is deleted.

06 / Questions

Questions.

Where is the log?

In an S3 bucket. Brokers keep no disks.

What assigns offsets?

Postgres.

Is the client Kafka?

Produce and Fetch. No consumer groups in v0.1.

Can a stale writer append?

No. A stale epoch cannot commit.

Where is the manual?

Docs. Sources are under Research.

WarpStream, AutoMQ, tiered storage?
